# ModuDose

**A youth-led, non-clinical medication-preparation support concept for young family caregivers and early-career care workers.**

Preparing several medicines can involve repeated sorting, checking and handoffs. ModuDose explores a clearer, more traceable workflow while keeping clinical decisions with qualified professionals.

## Project status

- A 12-compartment digital design and software workflow simulation have been completed.
- The simulation covers task-to-cup traceability, dispensing events, human review and exception handling. Missing or conflicting evidence is rejected in the simulated workflow.
- Physical dispensing has not yet been validated. Hardware integration and supervised inert-sample testing are next steps.
- Automatic dose packaging and label verification are future development goals, not current capabilities.

## Youth health and well-being

The project aims to reduce repeated handling and uncertainty for young family caregivers and support clearer care handoffs. These are intended benefits to be evaluated with young people and community partners; they have not yet been demonstrated.

## Planned seed-grant use (USD 5,000)

- $1,000 — safety and reliability testing
- $500 — accessible multilingual guidance
- $500 — supervised pilot materials

This initial budget supports dispensing validation and youth co-design. Full automated packaging would require a later scope and separate funding.

## Safety and evidence boundary

ModuDose does not diagnose, prescribe or change doses. Current evidence is digital design and software simulation only. Any physical testing will use inert samples under suitable supervision. The project makes no clinical-effectiveness claim.
